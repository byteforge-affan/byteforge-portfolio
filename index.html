About Me

The person
behind the screen

I'm Muhammad Affan — a CS student with a genuine interest in frontend development. I got into it because I liked seeing ideas turn into real things on screen. Over time that curiosity became something more disciplined: I stopped following tutorials and started building things from scratch, on my own terms.

What I care about most is the craft. A working website that looks rough is still a rough website. So I pay attention to spacing, hierarchy, motion, and the small details that most people don't notice — until something feels off.

I'm currently in my CS degree, working on projects in parallel, and looking for an environment where I can do real work and grow alongside people who hold themselves to a high standard. If that's where you are, let's connect.
